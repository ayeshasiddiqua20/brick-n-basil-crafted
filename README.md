# Brick & Basil Digital

"Build a high-converting, accessible web app for 'Brick & Basil', an authentic Italian fine-dine pizzeria in Davangere (S S Layout B Block). Use a warm, rustic aesthetic with terracotta brick tones (#8B3A2B), rich basil green (#2D5A27), and a warm cream background (#FAF7F2), paired with serif headings and clean sans-serif text.

Key Sections & Mechanics:

Hero Section: High-impact heading reading 'Artisanal Wood-Fired Pizzeria in Davangere'. Include a 4.7★ Rating badge (180+ Google Reviews) and two prominent CTA buttons: 'Reserve a Table' (Primary filled button opening a booking modal) and 'View Menu'.

Featured Artisanal Menu: Include categories for Wood-Fired Pizzas, Starters (Highlighting 'Peri Peri Crispy Oyster Mushrooms' and 'Artisanal Garlic Bread'), Fresh Basil Pasta, and Creamy Cheesecakes. Add dietary badges ('Vegetarian', 'Vegan Options').

Instagram & Ambiance Gallery Grid: A responsive image gallery layout styled for high-resolution Instagram posts (@brick_n_basil) showcasing rooftop sunset dining, wood-fired oven craft, and modern aesthetic interiors.

Verified Customer Testimonials: A carousel card section featuring real reviews:

'First of its kind in Davangere. The ambience is so well thought out for sunset and late evenings... felt a Bangalore vibe here!'

'The chef is a young artisanal baker. Premium cheese, no bloating, and the cheesecake is to die for!'

'Do not miss the peri peri crispy oyster mushrooms and artisanal pizzas!'

Quick Utility & Location Bar: A sticky section displaying one-tap Google Maps directions to S S Layout B Block, opening hours ('1:00 PM – 10:00 PM, Tuesday Closed'), and amenities ('Rooftop Seating', 'Free Street Parking').

Animations: Subtle scroll fades, smooth food card lifts, and clean modal transitions. Keep the experience warm, accessible, and conversion-driven."*
